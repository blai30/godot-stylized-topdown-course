## What you will build

Bushes: low, round clumps of leaves that fill the gaps between the trees and give the meadow some middle height. You will not model anything and paint nothing. A bush is the oak's own leafy crown, squashed to a third of its height and set on the ground with no trunk under it.

Three bushes by hand in the meadow, and then a rule so the generated level grows them in its groves.

## The scene

You made the oak's scene from its `.glb` in lesson 24. The bush starts the same way, from the same model, and changes only what the bush needs.

<Steps>

1. In the FileSystem dock make a folder `res://art/nature/bush`.

2. Choose **Scene > New Inherited Scene...** and pick `art/nature/tree_oak/tree_oak.glb`. Godot names the root `tree_oak2`, because the model already has a child called `tree_oak`. It has two children, `leaves` and `tree_oak`, the crown and the trunk. Rename the root `Bush`.

	<Figure
		src={inheritDialog}
		alt="The Open Base Scene dialog with art/nature/tree_oak open and tree_oak.glb selected."
		caption="The same model as the oak."
	/>

3. Select the `tree_oak` node, the trunk, and turn **Visible** off in the Inspector. A bush has no trunk. The mesh stays in the scene, hidden, because a node that comes from the imported model cannot be deleted in an inherited scene.

4. Select `leaves`. Set **Material Override** to `art/nature/tree_oak/leaves.tres`, the oak's leaf material, so the bush takes the same colors, the wind, and the cloud shadows without a second material to keep in step. Under **Transform** set **Scale** to `0.5, 0.35, 0.5` and **Position** to `0, -0.51, 0`.

	The oak's crown starts 1.45 meters above its origin, and the crown is squashed to 0.35 of its height, so it must move down by 1.45 times 0.35, about 0.51, for its underside to sit on the ground. The crown is also squashed to half its width, so a bush is about 2.4 meters across and 1.4 meters tall. The oak's crown measures about 4.7 meters across and 4.1 meters tall in the model, and the scale takes it from there.

	<Figure
		src={crown}
		alt="The crown alone in the 3D viewport, a flat round clump of leaves just above the grid."
		caption="The squashed crown, with the trunk hidden."
	/>

</Steps>

## The body and the wind

<Steps>

1. Select the root and add a **StaticBody3D** named `Body`, and under it a **CollisionShape3D** named `Shape`. Give `Shape` a **New CylinderShape3D** with **Radius** `0.7` and **Height** `1.0`, and set its **Position** to `0, 0.5, 0`. The cylinder is smaller than the crown on purpose: the player can brush through the outer leaves, and cannot walk through the middle.

2. Select the root and set its **Script** to `src/World/WindPhase.cs`, with Quick Load, as for the trees in lesson 28. It gives each bush its own phase in the wind, so a row of them does not sway in step. A bush leans much less than a tree, because the foliage shader's lean grows with height above the ground; the leaves flutter as much as the oak's.

## Plant three

<Steps>

1. Open `levels/meadow.tscn`, select the root `Meadow`, and add a **Node3D** named `Bushes`, beside `Trees` and `Rocks`.

2. With `Bushes` selected, click **Instantiate Child Scene** in the Scene dock and pick `bush.tscn`. Do it three times. Select each and set it in the Inspector:

	| Node | Position | Rotation Y | Scale |
	|---|---|---|---|
	| `Bush` | `-3, 0, 6` | `0` | `1` |
	| `Bush2` | `-1, 0, 7` | `120` | `0.8` |
	| `Bush3` | `0.5, 0, 6.2` | `250` | `0.9` |

	`Bush2` and `Bush3` are turned and resized, which keeps three copies of one model from looking like copies.

	<Figure

3. Save the scene and run it with **Play Scene**, <Kbd keys="F6" mac="Cmd+R" />.

The bushes cast soft shadows on the grass and take the cloud shadows from lesson 53 like the trees.

## A rule for the generated level

The generator grows what its profile's rules say, and the bush is one more rule.

<Steps>

1. Open `world/generation/generation_profile.tres`. Open its **Rules** array. It holds the oak and the pine rules from lesson 51. Set its **Size** to `3`, and in the new, empty third element choose **New PropRule**. Open the element and set:

	- **Scene** to `art/nature/bush/bush.tscn`;
	- **Weight** to `2`, so a bush is chosen two times in seven;
	- **Spacing** to `1.5` and **Shore Distance** to `1.5`, smaller than a tree's `2`, because a bush is small and may stand closer to its neighbors and to the water.

	Leave **Starts At** and **Full At** as they are: bushes grow in the same groves as the trees.

	<Figure
		src={rule}
		alt="The Inspector on the generation profile: Rules has three elements, and the third, a PropRule, has the bush scene, a weight of 2, spacing 1.5, and shore distance 1.5."
		caption="The third rule."
	/>

2. Open `levels/generated.tscn`, select `WorldGenerator`, and click **Generate**. The generator clears the props it made before and grows new ones, now with bushes among them.

	<Figure
		src={generated}
		alt="The generated level in the editor with oaks, pines, and bushes in its groves."
		caption="Generate, with the bush rule."
	/>

3. Save the scene.

</Steps>

In the verified run, with seed 15 the level had 86 props: 28 oaks, 17 pines, and 41 bushes. Bushes are more than their share of the weight because their spacing and shore distance are smaller, so more of them fit. The closest two props were 1.52 meters apart, none was in water, and pressing **Generate** again gave exactly the same props. The rules do what they did for the trees; nothing in the generator changed.

## Try it

- Change the bush's **Weight** to `0.5` for a rare bush, or `6` for a meadow thick with them.
- Change the crown's **Scale** to `0.7, 0.5, 0.7` for a big shrub.
- Change the leaf color for the bush alone: make the material unique on `leaves` and change **Leaf Color**. It then no longer follows the oak. The optional bonus lesson 65 shows a better way, a color that is set on each copy.
- Set the rules' **Size** to `4` and add a second bush rule with a **Starts At** of `-0.5`, so some bushes grow out in the open meadow and not only in groves.

## If it does not work

- **The bush has a trunk sticking out.** The `tree_oak` node is still visible. Turn **Visible** off.
- **The bush floats.** **Position** on `leaves` is not `0, -0.51, 0`.
- **The bush is as tall as an oak.** **Scale** is on the root and not on `leaves`, or it is `1`.
- **The leaves are plain, not the oak's.** `leaves` has no **Material Override**. Set it to `leaves.tres`.
- **The player walks through the bush.** `Body` has no shape, or the shape's radius is `0`.
- **Generate makes no bushes.** The rule's **Scene** is empty, or **Weight** is `0`.
