## What you will build

The character's **armature**: a skeleton of 22 bones, named the way Godot's humanoid profile names them. The skeleton does not change how the character looks. It is what lesson 40 attaches the mesh to, and what an animation moves.

<Figure
	src={display}
	alt="Blender's front view with the character's skeleton drawn in front of it, each bone labeled: Head, Neck, UpperChest, Chest, Spine, Hips, shoulders, upper and lower arms, hands, upper and lower legs, feet, and toes."
	caption="The finished skeleton, with names shown."
/>

## Why the names matter

An **animation** is a set of tracks, and each track says "rotate the bone called this". An animation made for a skeleton can move any other skeleton whose bones have the same names. The names in this lesson are the ones in Godot's **SkeletonProfileHumanoid**, the list Godot uses to **retarget**: to take an animation made for one body and play it on another. That is why lesson 42 can play the Universal Animation Library's walk and run on a character you modeled. Lesson 41 shows the map between your names and the library's.

The profile names 56 bones. A top-down character needs only 22 of them:

| Part | Bones |
|---|---|
| Spine | `Hips`, `Spine`, `Chest`, `UpperChest`, `Neck`, `Head` |
| Each arm | `Shoulder`, `UpperArm`, `LowerArm`, `Hand` |
| Each leg | `UpperLeg`, `LowerLeg`, `Foot`, `Toes` |

There are no fingers, because the hands are mitts, no eyes or jaw, because the face is painted, and no `Root` bone, because the game moves the character by moving its body, not by animating a bone.

## The spine

A bone is a line from a **head** to a **tail**. The bone's head is where it pivots. You will type each bone's name and its ends.

<Steps>

1. Click the bone's name field at the top of the Bone tab, press <Kbd keys="Ctrl+A" mac="Cmd+A" /> to select the old name, and type `Hips`. Set **Head** to `0`, `0`, `0.9` and **Tail** to `0`, `0`, `1`.

2. Hover the viewport and press <Kbd keys="E" /> and <Kbd keys="Enter" />: this **extrudes** a new bone from the tail of the last, **connected** to it, so its head starts where the last bone's tail is. Rename the new bone `Spine` and set only its **Tail** to `0`, `0`, `1.12`.

3. Do the same for the rest of the spine, one bone at a time:

	| Bone | Tail `Z` |
	|---|---|
	| `Chest` | `1.27` |
	| `UpperChest` | `1.40` |
	| `Neck` | `1.52` |
	| `Head` | `1.80` |

	<Figure
		src={spine}
		alt="The front view in Edit Mode with a chain of bones running up the middle of the character from the hips to the top of the head, and the Bone tab showing Head with Head Z 1.52 and Tail Z 1.8."
		caption="The spine: six bones in a chain."
	/>

</Steps>

The `Head` bone's head, its pivot, is at the base of the skull, at the chin, `1.52`, and its tail is the top of the head. The hips bone starts at the top of the legs. A joint of the body is wherever one bone's tail meets another's head.

## The left arm

The arm does not start from the end of the spine, but from the side of the chest. A bone that starts somewhere else needs to be a **new** bone, with the parent you choose.

<Steps>

1. Hover the viewport and press <Kbd keys="Shift+A" />. A new bone appears at the origin. Click it to make it the active bone. Rename it `LeftShoulder`, and set **Head** to `0`, `0`, `1.4` and **Tail** to `0.2`, `0`, `1.365`. The shoulder bone runs from the base of the neck to the shoulder's edge.

2. Open the **Relations** panel on the Bone tab. Its **Parent** field says which bone this one hangs from, and **Connected** whether its head is fixed to the parent's tail. Click the **Parent** field and choose `UpperChest`.

	<Figure
		src={relations}
		alt="The Bone tab with the Relations panel open: Parent is empty, Connected is off, and Local Location and Inherit Rotation are on."
		caption="The Relations panel."
	/>

	<Figure
		src={parent}
		alt="The Parent field's list open, with the bones Hips, Spine, Chest, UpperChest, Neck, and Head."
		caption="Choose the parent from the list."
	/>

3. With the shoulder active, extrude the rest of the arm with <Kbd keys="E" /> and <Kbd keys="Enter" />, renaming each bone and setting its tail:

	| Bone | Tail `X`, `Y`, `Z` |
	|---|---|
	| `LeftUpperArm` | `0.295`, `0`, `1.163` |
	| `LeftLowerArm` | `0.391`, `0`, `0.962` |
	| `LeftHand` | `0.432`, `0`, `0.855` |

	The arm bones follow the arm of the mesh: the same line you extruded in lesson 37.

	<Figure
		src={arm}

		caption="The left arm."
	/>

</Steps>

## The left leg

The leg also hangs from a point that is not an end of the chain: the side of the hips.

<Steps>

1. Press <Kbd keys="Shift+A" /> for a new bone, click it, and rename it `LeftUpperLeg`. Set **Head** to `0.1`, `0`, `0.9` and **Tail** to `0.1`, `0`, `0.5`. In **Relations**, set **Parent** to `Hips`.

2. Extrude the rest of the leg:

	| Bone | Tail `X`, `Y`, `Z` |
	|---|---|
	| `LeftLowerLeg` | `0.1`, `0`, `0.12` |
	| `LeftFoot` | `0.1`, `-0.1`, `0.05` |
	| `LeftToes` | `0.1`, `-0.2`, `0.03` |

	The foot and toes point forward, toward -Y, the way the character faces.

	<Figure
		src={leg}
		alt="The front view with the left leg's bones, from the hip to the toes, on the right of the picture."
		caption="The left leg."
	/>

</Steps>

## The right side

<Steps>

1. Hover the viewport and press <Kbd keys="A" /> to select every bone. Press <Kbd keys="F3" />, type `symmetrize`, and press <Kbd keys="Enter" />. **Symmetrize** copies the bones on one side of the center line to the other, and changes `Left` to `Right` in their names. It also keeps the parents: `RightShoulder` hangs from `UpperChest`, and `RightUpperLeg` from `Hips`.

	<Figure
		src={symmetrizeSearch}

## Try it

- Select a bone in Edit Mode and press <Kbd keys="G" />. Move it, and look at the tail of its parent: connected bones move together.
- Hide the mesh (<Kbd keys="H" /> over `body` in Object Mode) and look at the skeleton alone. Could you tell it is a person?
- Open the Bone tab for `LeftHand` and look at its **Roll**. Roll turns a bone around its own length. It matters when a bone twists, and the animations in lesson 42 are made for bones whose rolls agree with the library's.

## If it does not work

- **A new bone is not the active one.** The Bone tab still shows the last bone. Click the new bone in the viewport, near its middle.
- **The Relations panel shows the wrong bone.** The Bone tab shows the active bone, and the new bone is not active yet. Click the new bone in the viewport.
- **Symmetrize puts the right side on the same side.** Some bones are not selected, or the center line is not at `X` 0. Select every bone and check that `Hips` starts at `X` 0.
- **A bone is `Bone.001` instead of the name you typed.** The rename did not take. Click the name field and type it again.
- **The bones are hidden inside the mesh.** Turn on **In Front** in the armature's Viewport Display.
