## Why a tree, and not a player

An **AnimationPlayer** plays one clip at a time and you tell it which. That is enough for a prop. A character needs to choose by what is happening, and to blend: the step from standing to jogging should be a few frames of both, not a jump. An **AnimationTree** reads clips from an AnimationPlayer and mixes them by rules you give it. The simplest rule is a **blend space**: a line of numbers with a clip at each point, and a **blend position** that picks where on the line to be. At a point, the clip plays alone. Between two points, the tree mixes both, in proportion to how near it is to each.

Here the line is speed in meters per second, the clips are `Idle` at 0, `Jog_Fwd` at 3.5 (the player's walking speed), and sprint at 6 (its sprint speed), and the blend position is the player's speed.

## Stretch the clips to the ground

A jog clip is a loop, made so that one foot is on the ground while the body goes past. If the body moves faster or slower than the clip assumes, the foot slides on the ground like a skater. The fix is to play the clip faster or slower until the foot stays where it is put.

A planted foot in a clip moves backward, relative to the body, at a certain speed, the clip's **stride speed**. The reference run measured it on this character by playing the clip and reading the foot's position over the part of the clip where the foot is planted. To measure your own, play the clip in the editor and note how far the planted foot moves back over how many seconds, or simply adjust the timeline until the feet look still. For this character the result was: the jog's stride speed is **5.04 m/s**, the same in both feet. The clip lasts 0.933 seconds. At 3.5 m/s the foot must go back at 3.5, so the clip must run slower by the ratio 3.5 over 5.04, or last 0.933 × 5.04 / 3.5 = **1.34 seconds**. At 6 m/s, the sprint's stride speed is about 5.0 on your character (its feet are less clean), and the clip lasts 0.667 s, so it must last 0.667 × 5.0 / 6 = **0.56 seconds**.

`length × stride speed ÷ speed`

<Steps>

1. Click the `Jog_Fwd` point in the blend space. Turn on **Use Custom Timeline**, and set **Timeline Length** to `1.344`. Set **Loop Mode** to **Linear**.

2. Click the `Sprint` point and set **Timeline Length** to `0.556`, and its **Loop Mode** to **Linear**. The idle needs nothing.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Another character, another number">
These two numbers belong to this skeleton and this library. A character with longer legs would have a longer stride speed for the same clip, and a different timeline. If your feet slide, change the length: shorter makes the foot go back faster. A little slide is hard to see from the game's camera, and it is a common trade in games.
</Aside>

Write the script so that:

- it has `using Godot;` and is in the namespace `TopDownTemplate.Player;`;

- a `private static readonly StringName` named `BlendPosition` holds `"parameters/blend_position"`, and a `private CharacterBody3D? _body` holds the body;
- `_Ready()` sets `_body` to `Owner?.GetParent() as CharacterBody3D`, with a comment: the character is a child of the player's body, so its owner's parent is the body. It then sets `Active = true`, with a comment: the tree is off in the editor, so a saved scene never keeps the pose of a clip, and on when the game runs;
- `_Process(double delta)` returns when `_body` is `null`. Otherwise it makes the speed from `new Vector2(_body.Velocity.X, _body.Velocity.Z).Length()`, with a comment: only the walk along the ground counts, because falling is not a stride, and calls `Set(BlendPosition, speed)`.

## Try it

- Change `PlayerMovement`'s **Speed** to `2` and watch the jog's feet slide: its clip is now too fast for the ground. Set it back to `3.5`.
- Add a point `Hit_Chest` to the blend space at `-1`, with **Min Space** `-1`, and see the clip play when the blend position is negative. What would you need to drive it?

## If it does not work

- **The character does not animate.** `Active` is off: the script's `_Ready()` turns it on. Check that the script is on the `AnimationTree` and the project is built.
- **The character slides around in a T-pose.** The tree's **Anim Player** is empty, or points to the wrong node. It must be `../AnimationPlayer`.
- **The legs move but the character slides forward.** The timeline lengths are off. Check the formula, and your clip's stride speed.
- **The character stays in the idle.** `_body` is `null`: the tree is not in the player scene. The character must be a child of the player's body.
- **The blend jumps.** Syncing is off in the blend space, or **Acceleration** on `PlayerMovement` is very high. Set the blend space's **Sync Mode** as above.
- **The clip names are wrong.** The names in the blend space must match the library's: `Idle`, `Jog_Fwd`, and `Sprint`.
